# Tidal gate source review — Segment 8

Segment 8 reviews the remaining user-supplied photographs `IMG_5376.jpeg`,
`IMG_5377.jpeg`, `IMG_5378.jpeg`, `IMG_5381.jpeg`, `IMG_5383.jpeg` and
`IMG_5386.jpeg`. Missing sequence numbers were not inferred or treated as
continuation evidence.

The seven resulting records are Sound of Jura, west of Islay, Sound of Islay,
West Loch Tarbert on Jura, Sound of Gigha, Sanda Sound mid-channel and close
west of the Mull of Kintyre. Sound of Jura remains one broad record: the
different Ruadh Sgeir timing is retained as a local exception and is not
silently averaged into the greater-Sound turn times.

All source values are approximate estimates, not definitive observations.
The operational projections exist only because the owner has authorised
visible planning assumptions. They retain the source-only record under
`sourceReview`, mark all modelled rates `approximate`, state every assumed
bearing, neap rate and zero-duration slack, and warn users to take every value
with a pinch of salt.

Notable assumptions are:

- West Loch Tarbert and Sound of Gigha have no photographed stream rate; the
  operational profile uses a conspicuous provisional 1 kn value.
- West of Islay, Sanda Sound and close west of the Mull lack neap rates; their
  profiles use an explicit half-spring estimate.
- Sound of Islay uses its broad up-to 5 kn narrows value in both regimes.
- Greenock's standard-port reference range is a provisional planning input of
  MHWS 3.4 m, MHWN 2.8 m, MLWN 1.0 m and MLWS 0.3 m above Chart Datum. It must
  be checked against current licensed data before navigation.

The joined spatial objects are naming representatives or a lighthouse point,
not surveyed gates, fairways, stream stations, safe-water positions, routes or
waypoints. Hazards and clipped directions remain inspection notes and are not
converted into automatic routing advice.
