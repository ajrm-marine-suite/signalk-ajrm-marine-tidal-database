# Tidal-gate source review: Segment 4

Review date: 2026-08-22. This review covers only candidates 7–8. The
user-supplied pilot-book photographs are evidence; this note records compact
facts and image provenance without reproducing publication passages. The
publication title, edition and page number are not visible in the photographs.

The visible heading-to-image index is:

- `IMG_5403.jpeg`: **Lismore to the Sound of Mull**. Its introductory text
  defines the Sound's southeast entrance between Rubha an Ridire on Morvern and
  Scallastle Point on Mull. Its separate north/south stream figures belong to
  the Lismore approach and are not either Segment 4 locus.
- `IMG_5404.jpeg`: **Sound of Mull**. Its Tides block contains both Segment 4
  timing loci, the Sound-wide rate partition and the northwest-duration note.

The photographs print offsets against Oban. The `HW` interpretation reuses the
notation key already source-checked and released in Segment 3B; no extra image
was opened during this review.

The two records are deliberately distinct even though their southeast-going
start is the same. The southeast record does not reuse `Duart Point`: the
photographs do not name Duart, and the legacy record mixes southeast-Sound
timing with Duart coordinates. The Calve record does not reuse generic `Sound
of Mull`: that legacy record has different timings, spring/neap rates and
modelled zero slack. Both legacy records remain visible and fail closed.

Neither new record assigns regime-neutral timings to spring/neap values, copies
a regional ceiling into a rate observation, treats stream duration as slack,
manufactures true bearings or constructs a three-mile-southeast coordinate.
