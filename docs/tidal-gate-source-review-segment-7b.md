## Verified Sound of Luing evidence

- The north-going stream begins `+270 min` springs and `+315 min` neaps; the
  south-going stream begins `-105 min` springs and `-60 min` neaps relative to
  HW Oban. The preceding sentence frames tide changes as average. The wording
  is stream **begins**, not an exact turn centre or slack centre, and no
  precision bound is supplied.
- The page gives one passage-level slack duration per regime: `15 min` springs
  and `60 min` neaps. It does not assign either duration to a turn or define it
  as centred, before, after or split around a turn.
- The rate description applies jointly to flood and ebb and varies spatially
  from `3 kn` springs at Ardluing to maxima of `7 kn` springs and `5 kn` neaps
  among the islands around Fladda. There is no Ardluing neap value, exact rate
  station or separate turn observation.
- The source gives only north/south direction labels, not true
  current-towards bearings. It supplies no supported time-domain flow curve or
  spring/neap reference-range interpolation and pairing rule.
- A flood race is localized north of Rubha Fiola and west of Ormsa and
  Belnahua. An eddy off Rubha na Lic lies on different sides between flood and
  ebb. Along the Lunga shore, local ebb may continue about one hour after flood
  begins in mid-channel, so a whole-passage simultaneous turn cannot be
  projected.
- The visible Dangers text identifies the drying Rubh Ard Luing at the
  southeast entrance, surrounding rocks, a berth instruction and the Ardluing
  south-cardinal buoy. Its top-right continuation also shows an incompletely
  captured drying obstruction associated with Belnahua, extending about three
  cables and drying `0.3 m`; feature and direction text is clipped. This is
  hazard context, not actionable geometry.
- The clipped initial Sound Directions gives only broad course-keeping cautions
  around overfalls/current and being set off course under sail in light winds.
  It supplies no complete route or manoeuvring rule. The following discrete
  Corryvreckan-focused paragraph is excluded as required.

## Verified Dorus Mòr evidence

- Both flood and ebb jointly reach `8 kn` springs and `6 kn` neaps, with their
  fastest rate within the first two hours. These are passage-wide shared
  values with no exact station or independent per-turn rate observations.
- Flood runs east-to-west through Dorus Mòr and its beginning is labelled
  north- or west-going: `+270 min` springs and `+315 min` neaps. Ebb runs
  west-to-east and its beginning is labelled south- or east-going: `-105 min`
  springs and `-60 min` neaps. The compound labels describe different reaches;
  they are not true current bearings.
- Prolonged strong west winds can advance the stated times considerably. No
  correction or precision bound is supplied, so none is an exact machine turn.
- The passage-level slack durations are again `15 min` springs and `60 min`
  neaps, with no individual-turn assignment or centred/before/after placement.
- Printed `191°` and `151°` values occur in clearing-mark instructions. They
  are not stream bearings and are not imported as true directions.
- Flood-specific branching, races, eddies and area turbulence are described
  around Reisa Mhic Phaidean, Craignish Point, Garbh Reisa, Reisa an t-Sruith
  and Coiresa. Ebb is strongest on the south side near Garbh Reisa; converging
  streams create overfalls southeast of Rubha na Una. Strong southerly wind
  can create heavy northwest-entrance overfalls and confused seas.
- The visible Dangers text identifies a submerged rock about half a mile
  northwest of Craignish Point toward Coiresa with `0.6 m` over it. Further
  rocks, clearing marks and Directions are clipped, so the hazard record is
  explicitly incomplete.

## Positive operational-eligibility assessment

Both candidates were checked positively against every current v2 operational
requirement and neither is enabled:

- the licensed points establish only broad named-water representatives, not
  exact gate/rate loci;
- all four regime/direction beginnings are attributable, but Sound calls them
  average and Dorus permits considerable unbounded wind-driven advance;
- direction labels are not true current-towards bearings, and Dorus clearing
  bearings are unrelated to stream direction;
- passage slack lacks per-turn assignment and centred/before/after placement;
- rate prose is shared across flood/ebb and localized only to a passage or
  named subareas, not exact gate-local turn observations;
- qualitative spatial variation, local asynchronous flow and “fastest within
  two hours” do not establish the supported zero-at-turn, midpoint-peak
  sinusoidal flow model;
- no supported reference-range interpolation, range pairing or out-of-range
  rule is stated;
- hazards are substantial, spatially/directionally variable and incompletely
  captured; and
- publication title, publisher, edition and target page identity are absent,
  with Dorus also lacking a complete visible primary heading.

Consequently every machine timing, bearing and slack field is `unavailable`,
`rateObservations` is empty, both calculation models are unavailable, and each
record has thirteen exact stable blockers in `readiness.reasons`. The effective
operational allow-list remains empty. Planning receives both native-v2 records
as display-only and cannot calculate from either new record or either preserved
legacy v1 record.
